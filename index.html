Classroomscreen Export

Version 1.2

Save your Classroomscreen boards as an image or a PDF, with the background, text and drawings. Your boards never leave your device: everything happens in your own browser.

On a laptop

  1. Show your bookmarks bar if it's hidden: Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar:
    Export board
  3. Open your board on classroomscreen.com, click an empty spot, then click Export board in the bookmarks bar.

On an Android tablet (Chrome or Brave)

  2. Bookmark this page: tap ⋮ at the top right, then the ☆ star.
  3. Open ⋮ → Bookmarks, tap ⋮ next to the new bookmark, then Edit. Change the name to Export board. In the address field, delete everything and paste the code. Save.
  4. Open your board on classroomscreen.com, tap the address bar, type Export board, and tap the suggestion with the star.

Using it

A small panel appears at the top right of your board. Drag it by its title bar to put it anywhere; it remembers the spot.

  • Save as image: one PNG of the screen you're on.
  • Add screen to PDF: do it on each screen, one after the other, then Download PDF.

Updates

When Classroomscreen changes its site, the export is fixed and a new version is published here. Your panel then says "Version … is out". Click that message, and replace your bookmark the same way you added it. On the laptop, delete the old bookmark and drag the new one in. On the tablet, edit the bookmark and paste the new code.

Good to know

  • Videos and embedded websites on a screen can't be captured and come out blank.
  • The export is checked against the real Classroomscreen site every day.
